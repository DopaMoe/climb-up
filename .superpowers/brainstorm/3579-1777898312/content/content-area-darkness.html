How dark should the admin content area be?

The sidebar will be deep navy. The question is what the main content area feels like.

CU

Full Dark

Everything stays deep navy/dark — sidebar and content area are the same darkness. Like VS Code, Linear at night. Very immersive and dramatic.

CU

Layered Dark

Near-black sidebar (darkest), slate content area (medium dark), navy cards (lighter dark). Depth through subtle layering — professional and premium.

CU

Dark Sidebar + Light Content

Classic split: deep navy sidebar, clean white/slate-50 content. Maximum contrast and readability. Familiar SaaS pattern — like Stripe Dashboard.
